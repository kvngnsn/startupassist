# Annual Compliance Calendar

For a Delaware C corporation with a December 31 fiscal year, a California headquarters (substitute the home state's items where different), employees on payroll, an equity plan, and investors. Each item lists the deadline, the authority, and the consequence of missing it. Use it with templates/compliance/delaware-franchise-tax-and-annual-report.md, templates/compliance/foreign-qualification-guide.md, and references/governance-and-compliance.md.

**How to use.** Copy the table into the company's calendar at the start of each year and assign an owner to each row. Federal deadlines that fall on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday move to the next business day (I.R.C. § 7503); state rules vary, so file state and Delaware items by the last business day before a weekend deadline unless the agency confirms otherwise. Items marked "if applicable" depend on the company's facts (state footprint, headcount, foreign ownership, Delaware tax size).

## Notes

- **Beneficial ownership reporting:** U.S.-formed corporations no longer file Corporate Transparency Act BOI reports after FinCEN's August 2026 final rule; banks still collect beneficial-ownership information under 31 C.F.R. § 1010.230. Do not calendar BOI filings for a Delaware corporation.
- **R&D tax items:** the § 174A small-business retroactive election deadline (the earlier of July 6, 2026 or the refund-claim deadline) has passed. For calendar-year companies that elected to deduct 2022–2024 unamortized R&E ratably over two years, the second half is deducted on the 2026 return. A § 41(h) payroll credit elected on a return filed during a quarter begins against payroll taxes in the next calendar quarter (Form 8974 with Form 941).
- **Sales tax:** review economic nexus annually (references/governance-and-compliance.md § 7.6).
- **Other headquarters states:** replace the California rows with the home state's income or franchise tax return, estimated payments, payroll filings, and annual report. New York (CT-3 April 15; biennial statement), Texas (May 15 report; no income tax), Washington (annual report; B&O returns monthly, quarterly, or annually as DOR assigns), and Massachusetts (excise return; annual report March 15) are the most common alternatives.

> **Verify:** Penalty amounts for federal information returns (§§ 6721–6722) are indexed annually; check the current year's IRS General Instructions for Certain Information Returns. Confirm the California pay data report due date, and each state's annual-report fee, before relying on this calendar for a given year.
