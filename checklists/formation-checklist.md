# Delaware C-Corporation Formation Checklist

Use this checklist to run a formation from first decision to first option grant. Steps are in the order they must happen. **Do not skip ahead**: most formation defects come from doing a later step (issuing founder stock, granting options, signing contracts as "the company") before an earlier one (bylaws adopted, board elected, consent effective).

Each step lists who acts or signs, the deadline, the authority, and the template in this skill. Example company: Northwind Robotics, Inc.; founders Alex Rivera (Texas) and Jordan Lee (California); 10,000,000 authorized shares of Common Stock, par $0.00001.

## Phase 0 — Decisions before filing

| # | Decision | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Entity type and state: Delaware C corporation | Founders | Venture investors, SAFEs and NVCA forms assume it; QSBS (§ 1202) requires a C corporation. |
| 0.2 | Equity split among founders | Founders | Record the split in the founder stock schedule (initial board consent Exhibit B). |
| 0.3 | Vesting: period, cliff, credit for prior work, acceleration | Founders | Market: 48 months, 12-month cliff, double-trigger acceleration. Investors will require vesting at the first priced round anyway. |
| 0.4 | Consideration: cash, technology, or both | Founders, counsel | Never "for services" (taxable compensation). Technology for stock raises § 351 questions; get tax input if valuable. |
| 0.5 | Authorized shares and par value | Counsel | Default 10,000,000 Common, $0.00001 par. Franchise tax: use the assumed par value capital method (minimum $400). |
| 0.6 | Board composition and officers | Founders | Initial directors (natural persons), officer titles matching Bylaws Article V. |
| 0.7 | Public benefit corporation? | Founders | If yes, add § 362 purpose and heading; confirm investor appetite. |
| 0.8 | IP inventory and conflicts | Each founder, counsel | List pre-formation technology (Schedule A of the technology assignment) and any employer, university, government-funding or open-source issues (Schedule C). Resolve conflicts before signing. |
| 0.9 | Headquarters state | Founders | Drives foreign qualification, payroll, tax and blue-sky filings. |

## Phase 1 — Create the corporation

| # | Step | Who acts / signs | Deadline / timing | Authority | Template |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Name check.** Search the Delaware Division of Corporations entity database (or ask the registered agent); run USPTO and common-law trademark searches; check availability in the headquarters state. Optional reservation: 120 days, $75. | Founders / counsel | Before filing | 8 Del. C. § 102(a)(1), (e); Division fee schedule | — |
| 1.2 | **Engage a registered agent** (commercial agent with a real Delaware office; no virtual-only agents). Provide a natural-person contact. | Officer-to-be / counsel | Before filing (the charter names the agent) | §§ 131, 132(a)–(d) | — |
| 1.3 | **Prepare and sign the certificate of incorporation.** Choose Alternative A (common only) and Alternative A of Article XII (incorporator elects directors). Include officer exculpation, written-ballot waiver, board bylaw power, optional § 242(b)(2) opt-out, forum selection. | Sole incorporator | — | §§ 101–103 | certificate-of-incorporation |
| 1.4 | **File the charter** through the registered agent or the Division's filing service. Fee: minimum $109 (one page; $9 per additional page; filing tax on authorized capital, minimum $15, included); certified copy $50 plus $2 per page; optional expedite (24-hour $50; same day $100; 2-hour $500; 1-hour $1,000). Record the **filing date and time** from the Division's stamp. | Incorporator / registered agent | Day 0 | § 103(c)–(d); § 391; Division fee schedule (Aug. 1, 2026) | — |
| 1.5 | **Action of sole incorporator**: adopt bylaws, fix board size, elect directors, resign. State a definite effective date and time **after** the filing time. | Sole incorporator | Day 0, after filing | §§ 107, 108(c), 109(a) | action-of-sole-incorporator; bylaws |
| 1.6 | **Initial board consent**: ratify; confirm bylaws; elect officers; principal office; fiscal year; uncertificated shares; registered agent; EIN; bank; expenses; foreign qualification; payroll registrations; founder stock (FMV and technology valuation); technology assignments; securities compliance (including California § 25102(f) if applicable); CIIAAs; indemnification agreements; optional plan adoption; franchise tax; BOI acknowledgment; general authority. Effective date and time **after** the incorporator action. **Every director signs.** | All directors | Day 0 (or later), after 1.5 | §§ 108(c), 141(f), 142, 151(f), 152, 153, 158 | initial-board-consent |
| 1.7 | **Secretary signs the Certificate of Adoption of Bylaws**; assemble the minute book (certified charter, incorporator action, bylaws, board consent). | Secretary | Same day as 1.6 | § 142(a) | bylaws |

## Phase 2 — Founder stock and IP (the 83(b) clock starts here)

| # | Step | Who acts / signs | Deadline / timing | Authority | Template |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Founder Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement** for each founder, with Stock Assignment Separate from Certificate (signed in blank), Joint Escrow Instructions (if escrow) or book-entry hold, and Spousal Consent (if married and in a community property or opt-in jurisdiction, or as Company policy). The RSPA closing date is the **issuance date** and the **§ 83 transfer date**. | Company officer (not the founder, where possible); founder; spouse | On or after 1.6 effective time; never before | 8 Del. C. §§ 152–153, 202; I.R.C. § 83 | founder-stock-purchase-agreement; spousal-consent |
| 2.2 | **Technology Assignment Agreement** for each founder who created pre-formation IP; deliver code, repositories, domains, accounts. Record patent and trademark assignments (USPTO Assignment Center) within 3 months; copyrights with the Copyright Office as needed. | Company officer; founder | Same day as 2.1 (it is consideration if Alternative B or C) | 35 U.S.C. § 261; 17 U.S.C. §§ 204, 205 | technology-assignment-agreement |
| 2.3 | **Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement** for each founder (and every later employee and contractor). | Company officer; founder | Same day as 2.1; before any post-formation work | State invention-assignment notice statutes (e.g., Cal. Lab. Code § 2870) | employment-and-contractors family |
| 2.4 | **Indemnification agreements** for each director and officer. | Company officer; each indemnitee | Same day as 1.6 or promptly after | 8 Del. C. § 145 | indemnification-agreement |
| 2.5 | **Receive consideration** (check or wire for cash; signed technology assignment). A check delivered at closing is payment even if deposited when the bank account opens. | CFO / Secretary | At RSPA closing | § 152 | — |
| 2.6 | **Stock ledger entries** and **§ 151(f) notices** with ROFR, repurchase, market stand-off and securities legends. | Secretary | Ledger: at issuance. Notice: within a reasonable time (best practice: within a few days) | §§ 151(f), 202(a), 219(c), 224 | stock-records |
| 2.7 | **Deliver the 83(b) package** to each founder with the deadline written on page one; diarize day 20. | Secretary / counsel | At RSPA signing | Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2 | 83b-election-package |
| 2.8 | **Founders file 83(b) elections** (Form 15620 Rev. April 2025 or a § 1.83-2(e) statement) by USPS Certified Mail, return receipt requested, to the IRS address for their state (Form 1040 "no payment" address), or online via IRS Online Account if the form accepts the figures. Copy to the Company with proof of mailing. **Not attached to the tax return.** | Each founder (taxpayer) | **Within 30 days after the RSPA closing date** (next business day if the 30th day is a weekend or legal holiday). Example: closing January 6, 2027 → deadline February 5, 2027. No extensions. | I.R.C. § 83(b)(2); Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2(b)–(e); §§ 7502, 7503 | 83b-election-package |
| 2.9 | **California § 25102(f) Limited Offering Exemption Notice** if any founder purchased in California: file electronically with DFPI; fee under Corp. Code § 25608(c) ($25 for $25,000 or less); consent to service of process for a non-California issuer; signed by an authorized officer, director or the issuer's attorney. Failure to file does not lose the exemption but triggers a late fee. | Officer or counsel | **Within 15 calendar days after the first sale in California** | Cal. Corp. Code § 25102(f); 10 Cal. Code Regs. § 260.102.14 | initial-board-consent § 16 |
| 2.10 | **Other states' securities exemptions** for founders residing elsewhere (many exempt sales to officers and directors or isolated transactions without filing). No federal Form D for a § 4(a)(2) founder issuance. | Counsel | Per state rule; generally before or shortly after sale | Securities Act § 4(a)(2); state blue-sky laws | — |

> **Verify:** Confirm the DFPI's current electronic filing system and fee schedule at the time of filing (the DFPI moved to a new system, FRANSES, in 2025; the § 25608(c) schedule was confirmed only through a mirror of the official code).

## Phase 3 — Tax ID, bank, records, and BOI

| # | Step | Who acts / signs | Deadline / timing | Authority | Template |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | **EIN** via the IRS online application (free; responsible party must have an SSN or ITIN; otherwise Form SS-4 by fax or mail). | Officer (responsible party) | Immediately after 1.6; needed for the bank | I.R.C. § 6109; IRS EIN guidance | — |
| 3.2 | **Bank account**: certified charter, bylaws or board resolutions, EIN letter (CP 575), officer IDs, beneficial-ownership certification for the bank. Deposit founders' purchase payments. | CEO / CFO | After 3.1 | 31 C.F.R. § 1010.230 | initial-board-consent § 10 |
| 3.3 | **Beneficial ownership information (BOI) report**: **none required** for a U.S.-formed corporation under FinCEN's August 2026 final rule. Foreign entities registered in the U.S. still report. Monitor for changes. | — | — | 31 C.F.R. § 1010.380 (as amended) | initial-board-consent § 22 |
| 3.4 | **Minute book and stock ledger** complete and reconciled to the cap table (and to any equity management platform). | Secretary | Within a week of formation; then ongoing | §§ 219(c), 224 | stock-records |
| 3.5 | **Organizational expenses**: reimburse founders with receipts; decide § 248 / § 195 treatment with the tax preparer. | CFO | First month | I.R.C. §§ 195, 248 | initial-board-consent § 11 |

## Phase 4 — Qualify and register where the company operates

| # | Step | Who acts / signs | Deadline / timing | Authority | Template |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | **Foreign qualification in the headquarters state** (and any other state where the company is "transacting business"). Typically requires a Delaware certificate of good standing. Examples: **California** — Statement and Designation by Foreign Corporation (Form S&DC-S/N) $100; Statement of Information (SI-550) $25 within 90 days and annually; $800 minimum franchise tax (a corporation that qualifies on or after January 1, 2000 is not subject to the minimum tax for its first taxable year, Rev. & Tax. Code § 23153(f)); "doing business" thresholds under Rev. & Tax. Code § 23101 (2025: sales $757,070, property or payroll $75,707, or 25% of totals). **New York** — Application for Authority $225. **Texas** — Application for Registration (Form 301) $750. **Washington** — foreign registration $180 plus online processing fee. | Officer / registered agent | Before transacting business in the state; promptly after formation if headquartered there | State foreign-corporation statutes | initial-board-consent § 12 |
| 4.2 | **State tax registrations**: income/franchise tax account; sales and use tax permit if selling taxable goods or services; local business licenses. | Officer / accountant | Before the activity begins | State law | initial-board-consent § 13 |
| 4.3 | **Payroll setup** before the first paycheck: payroll provider; federal and state withholding accounts; state unemployment insurance registration; workers' compensation insurance; Form W-4 and state equivalents; Form I-9 within three business days after an employee starts (8 C.F.R. § 274a.2(b)(1)(ii)); new-hire reporting (federal outer limit 20 days, 42 U.S.C. § 653a(b)(2); some states shorter). Founders who are officers and receive salary are employees. | CFO / payroll provider | Before first payroll | Federal and state employment law | employment-and-contractors family |

> **Verify:** Check the 2026 California § 23101 "doing business" thresholds (the FTB had posted figures only through 2025 when this checklist was verified), Massachusetts foreign-registration fees (not verified), and each state's registration deadline for unemployment insurance (California's EDD, for example, requires registration within 15 calendar days after paying more than $100 in wages in a calendar quarter).

## Phase 5 — Equity plan, valuation, first grants

| # | Step | Who acts / signs | Deadline / timing | Authority | Template |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | **Adopt the equity incentive plan** with a stated share reserve (never blank); confirm founder shares + reserve ≤ authorized shares. | Board | Before any grant (often in 1.6) | 8 Del. C. §§ 152, 157; I.R.C. § 422(b) | equity-compensation family; initial-board-consent § 19 |
| 5.2 | **Stockholder approval of the plan** (needed for incentive stock options). | Stockholders (written consent) | Within 12 months before or after board adoption | I.R.C. § 422(b)(1); 8 Del. C. § 228 | equity-compensation family |
| 5.3 | **§ 409A valuation** of the Common Stock by an independent appraiser (safe-harbor presumption); refresh at least every 12 months and after material events (financings, significant revenue changes). | Board / CFO | Before the first option grant priced above par-era values | Treas. Reg. § 1.409A-1(b)(5)(iv)(B) | equity-compensation family |
| 5.4 | **First grants**: board (or delegated committee/officer under §§ 152(b), 157(c)) approves each grant with exercise price ≥ FMV on the grant date; grant notices and option agreements; Rule 701 compliance (disclosure required if more than $10 million is sold in a 12-month period); California § 25102(o) notice if California participants. | Board; grantees | At or after 5.1–5.3 | 17 C.F.R. § 230.701; Cal. Corp. Code § 25102(o) | equity-compensation family |

> **Verify:** Confirm the timing and fee for the California § 25102(o) notice (filed with the DFPI after the first issuance under the plan to a California resident) at the time of the first grant.

## Phase 6 — Recurring obligations calendar

| When | Obligation | Who | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Each issuance or transfer | Stock ledger entry and § 151(f) notice | Secretary | §§ 151(f), 224 |
| Day 20 after any restricted stock issuance | Confirm the 83(b) was mailed; collect proof | Secretary | Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2(b) |
| June 1, September 1, December 1 | Delaware estimated franchise tax installments (40% / 20% / 20%) if annual tax is $5,000 or more | CFO | Division franchise tax page |
| **March 1 annually** | Delaware annual franchise tax report and tax (compute under the assumed par value capital method; $50 report fee); late: $200 penalty plus 1.5% monthly interest; void after one year of non-payment | CFO / Secretary | 8 Del. C. §§ 502–504, 510 |
| Annually (calendar chosen by bylaws) | Election of directors by annual meeting or written consent in lieu of meeting | Stockholders | 8 Del. C. § 211(b)–(c) |
| Annually | California Statement of Information (SI-550) and other states' annual reports | Secretary | State law |
| At least every 12 months | Refresh § 409A valuation before new grants | Board / CFO | Treas. Reg. § 1.409A-1(b)(5)(iv)(B) |
| Federal corporate return due date | Form 1120 (calendar-year corporations: 15th day of the 4th month after year end) and state returns; maintain gross-asset records for § 1202 | CFO / accountant | I.R.C. §§ 6072(a), 1202(d) |
| On any founder departure | Repurchase option analysis and exercise within the RSPA window (default 90 days) | Board / Secretary | RSPA § 4 | 

## Formation defect checklist (stop and fix before any financing)

- [ ] Certificate of incorporation filed; certified copy in minute book.
- [ ] Incorporator action signed with a definite effective date and time, after filing; bylaws attached.
- [ ] Board consent signed by every director, with a definite effective date and time after the incorporator action; no "effective when signed by all parties" language.
- [ ] No stock issued before the board consent's effective time; no stock issued "for services"; every issuance approved by the board with a stated price and consideration.
- [ ] Bylaws (with ROFR) adopted before any stock was issued; § 151(f) notices delivered with legends.
- [ ] Equity plan reserve stated as a number; total issued + reserved ≤ authorized.
- [ ] Each founder's 83(b) filed within 30 days with proof of mailing on file.
- [ ] Technology assignment and CIIAA signed by every founder; IP conflicts resolved.
- [ ] Spousal consents where required.
- [ ] California § 25102(f) notice filed (if applicable).
- [ ] No stockholder consent purporting to "elect" directors retroactively after the board had already been acting.
- [ ] Delaware franchise tax and annual report current; registered agent active; good standing available.

If any box cannot be checked, use the § 204 ratification template (governance family) rather than backdating or re-papering. See references/delaware-corporate-law.md, Section 9.
