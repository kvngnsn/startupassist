## About this template

**What it does.** When a founder leaves, the founder stock purchase agreement (the "RSPA") gives the Company an option to buy back the founder's unvested shares, usually at the lower of the original price and current fair market value. This file contains (1) the Board approval and (2) the notice to the departing founder exercising the option, plus an escrow-agent instruction. Done properly and on time, the unvested shares return to the Company for a few dollars; done late or sloppily, the departed founder may keep them.

**Step-by-step.**
1. **Fix the Termination Date.** Use the date service actually ended (resignation effective date, termination date in the separation letter). If the founder continues as a director or consultant, service may not have ended at all: under the RSPA, Continuous Service Status continues through a change in capacity. Confirm in writing what capacities ended.
2. **Check acceleration.** Read RSPA Section 3.3. If double-trigger acceleration applies and the termination could be an "Involuntary Termination" (termination without Cause or resignation for Good Reason), determine whether a Change in Control occurred or may occur within the window. If the termination occurred before a possible Change in Control, the RSPA defers exercise until the pre-closing portion of the window passes. If single-trigger acceleration applies, confirm no Change in Control has closed.
3. **Count the shares.** Compute vested and unvested shares as of the Termination Date under RSPA Section 3.1 (cliff, monthly vesting, rounding, any prior-service credit, any leave-of-absence suspension). Recompute independently of the cap-table software and reconcile.
4. **Determine the price.** Repurchase Price per share = lower of (a) the original Purchase Price (as adjusted for splits) and (b) Fair Market Value on the Termination Date, as determined by the Board (consider the latest § 409A valuation). Multiply by the unvested shares.
5. **Confirm legal capacity to repurchase.** § 160(a)(1) prohibits a repurchase for cash or other property when the Company's capital is impaired or would be impaired. At par-value prices this is rarely a problem, but the Board must make the determination; directors who approve an unlawful repurchase can be jointly and severally liable under § 174. RSPA Section 4.8 extends the exercise period if the Company is legally prohibited.
6. **Mind the deadline.** Under Alternative A of RSPA Section 4.3, the notice must be delivered within the exercise period (default 90 days after the Termination Date). Under Alternative B (automatic exercise), the repurchase happens on the deadline unless the Company declines; use the "confirmation" version of the notice.
7. **Approve.** The Board approves by unanimous written consent (§ 141(f)) or at a meeting. **If the departing founder is still a director**, a unanimous written consent requires that founder's signature, which may not come. Use a meeting instead: give notice under Bylaws Section 3.8, count the founder toward the quorum (§ 144(d)(1)), have the disinterested directors approve after disclosure (§ 144(a)(1)), and record the founder's recusal. If a majority of the directors are not disinterested, § 144(a)(1) requires approval by a committee of at least two disinterested directors for the safe harbor; on a small board where that is impossible, document that the repurchase follows the contract's fixed formula (which supports fairness under § 144(a)(3)). Exercise of a contract right at a formula price is low-risk, but do it cleanly.
8. **Deliver the notice** by a method permitted by RSPA Section 15.4 (email plus overnight courier is prudent) to the founder's last address and email on file, and to the spouse if a spousal consent was signed.
9. **Pay** at closing by check, wire, or cancellation of indebtedness (RSPA Section 4.4). If the founder refuses payment, tender or deposit it (RSPA Section 4.4 makes the repurchase effective anyway).
10. **Complete the transfer.** Escrow agent (or Secretary, under the book-entry alternative) completes the founder's pre-signed stock assignment; the Company updates the stock ledger, retires the shares or holds them as treasury shares, and sends the founder a new § 151(f) notice for the remaining vested shares.
11. **Tax notes.** If the founder made an 83(b) election, the founder's loss on the forfeited shares is limited to the amount paid minus the amount realized (Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2(a)) — at par, negligible. If the founder did not make the election, the unvested shares were never treated as transferred for tax purposes, and the repurchase is generally a non-event for the founder. The Company has no withholding obligation on a repurchase at cost.
12. **Retired or treasury.** Retiring the shares (§ 243) reduces issued shares, which slightly increases the assumed par value under the Delaware franchise tax's assumed-par-value-capital method; holding them as treasury shares keeps them in the "issued shares" count for that method (treasury shares count as issued for that calculation). The difference is usually immaterial; many companies retire. Repurchased founder shares do not automatically return to the equity plan reserve; add them to the reserve only by plan amendment with any required stockholder approval.

**Common mistakes.** Missing the 90-day window; no Board action; wrong share count (forgetting the cliff or credit for prior service); asking the departing founder to sign the board consent as a director; not paying the price; not updating the ledger and § 151(f) notice; overlooking double-trigger acceleration in a sale process.

## Repurchase of Unvested Shares from {{founder_name}}

WHEREAS, the Company and {{founder_name}} (the "**Founder**") are parties to a Founder Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement dated {{rspa_date}} (the "**RSPA**"), under which the Founder purchased {{founder_shares}} shares of the Company's Common Stock (the "**Shares**") subject to vesting and to an option of the Company to repurchase Unvested Shares (as defined in the RSPA) upon termination of the Founder's Continuous Service Status (the "**Repurchase Option**");

WHEREAS, the Founder's Continuous Service Status terminated on {{termination_date}} (the "**Termination Date**"), and the Founder no longer provides services to the Company in any capacity [[Optional: other than as a director of the Company, which the parties have agreed does not continue Continuous Service Status because {{continued_service_explanation}}]];

WHEREAS, the Board has reviewed the vesting provisions of the RSPA and has determined that, as of the Termination Date, {{vested_shares}} Shares were Vested Shares and {{unvested_shares}} Shares were Unvested Shares (the "**Repurchase Shares**");

WHEREAS, the Board has determined that no acceleration of vesting under Section 3.3 of the RSPA applies to the Repurchase Shares, because [[Alternative A: the RSPA provides for no acceleration]][[Alternative B: no Change in Control (as defined in the RSPA) has occurred]][[Alternative C: the termination was not an Involuntary Termination (as defined in the RSPA)]][[Alternative D: no Change in Control has occurred, and the portion of the Change in Control Period preceding a Change in Control (as defined in the RSPA) has expired without one]];

WHEREAS, the Board has determined in good faith that the Fair Market Value (as defined in the RSPA) of a share of Common Stock on the Termination Date was {{fmv_per_share}}, so that the Repurchase Price under Section 4.2 of the RSPA is {{repurchase_price_per_share}} per share, being the lower of the Purchase Price of {{price_per_share}} per share and that Fair Market Value, for an aggregate Repurchase Price of {{aggregate_repurchase_price}};

WHEREAS, the Board has reviewed the Company's most recent balance sheet and other information it considers reliable, and has determined that the capital of the Company is not impaired and that the repurchase of the Repurchase Shares for the aggregate Repurchase Price will not cause any impairment of the capital of the Company, within the meaning of Section 160(a)(1) of the DGCL; and

WHEREAS, the Board has determined that exercising the Repurchase Option is in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Company exercises the Repurchase Option as to all of the Repurchase Shares at the Repurchase Price, on the terms of the RSPA;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Notice of Exercise of Repurchase Option in substantially the form presented to the Board (the "**Notice**") is approved, and the officers of the Company are authorized and directed to sign the Notice and deliver it to the Founder (and to the Founder's spouse or domestic partner, if a spousal consent was signed) in accordance with Section 15.4 of the RSPA on or before {{exercise_deadline}};

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the officers are authorized and directed to pay the aggregate Repurchase Price to the Founder at the closing of the repurchase by {{payment_method}}, and, if the Founder cannot be located or refuses payment, to tender or deposit it as provided in Section 4.4 of the RSPA;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that [[Alternative A: the Escrow Agent under the Joint Escrow Instructions attached to the RSPA is directed to complete and deliver the Stock Assignment Separate from Certificate held in escrow, and any certificate representing the Repurchase Shares, to the Company at the closing]][[Alternative B: the Secretary is authorized and directed, under Section 5 of the RSPA, to complete the Stock Assignment Separate from Certificate held by the Company and to transfer the Repurchase Shares to the Company on its stock ledger at the closing]];

RESOLVED FURTHER, that, upon their repurchase, the Repurchase Shares shall be [[Alternative A: retired and restored to the status of authorized but unissued shares of Common Stock, and the capital of the Company shall be reduced as provided in Sections 243 and 244 of the DGCL]][[Alternative B: held by the Company as treasury shares]];

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Secretary is directed to record the repurchase in the Company's stock ledger and to deliver to the Founder a notice under Section 151(f) of the DGCL reflecting the {{vested_shares}} Vested Shares the Founder continues to hold, which remain subject to the restrictions on transfer in the RSPA and the Bylaws; and
