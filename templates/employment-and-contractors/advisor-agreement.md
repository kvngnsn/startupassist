## About this template

**What it does.** An advisor agreement formalizes a light-touch relationship: an experienced individual gives advice, introductions, and occasional review in exchange for a small, vesting equity award. The agreement does three legal jobs that an email or handshake does not: (1) it assigns to the Company any intellectual property the Advisor creates in the course of advising, (2) it imposes confidentiality, and (3) it fixes the equity terms so that the grant can be documented, approved by the Board, and priced at fair market value. This is an original form; it borrows the *concept* popularized by the Founder Institute's FAST template (equity calibrated to company stage and engagement level, short vesting) but not its text.

**Where it sits.** Sign the Advisor Agreement first; then the Board approves the equity grant (by unanimous written consent under 8 Del. C. § 141(f) or at a meeting) under the equity incentive plan, and the Company and Advisor sign the plan's standard restricted stock purchase agreement or stock option agreement. The Agreement itself only *recommends* the grant; it never promises a grant price, because the exercise or purchase price must equal fair market value on the grant date, which is determined by the Board when it approves the grant.

**Statutory requirements.**
- *Securities law.* Equity issued to advisors is usually exempt under Rule 701, which covers compensatory issuances to consultants and advisors only if they are **natural persons**, provide **bona fide services**, and the services are **not in connection with the offer or sale of securities in a capital-raising transaction** and do not promote or maintain a market for the Company's securities (17 C.F.R. § 230.701(c)(1)). Do not grant advisor equity to an advisor's LLC (grant to the individual), and do not compensate an advisor for introducing investors (that also raises unregistered broker-dealer issues under Exchange Act § 15(a)). For California-resident advisors, the grant must also be exempt under Cal. Corp. Code § 25102(o) or another state exemption; the plan drafter handles the notice filing.
- *Tax.* Advisors are not employees, so they cannot receive incentive stock options (I.R.C. § 422(a)(2)); options are non-qualified (NSOs). An option is exempt from § 409A only if its exercise price is not less than fair market value of the underlying stock on the grant date (Treas. Reg. § 1.409A-1(b)(5)(i)(A)). Restricted stock that vests is taxed under § 83(a) on vesting unless the Advisor files an § 83(b) election within **30 days** after the stock is transferred (Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2(b)). The IRS's optional Form 15620 may be used for the election.
- *DTSA notice.* Advisors are "employees" for DTSA notice purposes because the term includes "any individual performing work as a contractor or consultant" (18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)(4)). The notice in Section 6.2 is required to preserve exemplary damages and fees against the Advisor.
- *Copyright.* Advice and documents an Advisor writes are not "works made for hire" (the Advisor is not an employee, and advisory work rarely fits the nine commissioned-work categories in 17 U.S.C. § 101), so ownership transfers only by a signed written assignment (17 U.S.C. § 204(a)). Section 7 uses present-tense assignment ("hereby assigns"). Do not add "work made for hire" language for a California advisor: under Cal. Lab. Code § 3351.5(c) and Cal. Unemp. Ins. Code § 686, a person who commissions a work made for hire under a written agreement is treated as the creator's *employer* for workers' compensation and unemployment insurance purposes.

> **Verify:** Advisor equity ranges are market practice, not law, and drift with the financing environment. Before recommending a number, check current benchmark data (e.g., Carta or law-firm advisor-equity surveys) and the Founder Institute's current FAST grid (Version 3 was published July 2026).

**Equity type: restricted stock vs. option.**
- *Restricted stock* (purchased at fair market value, subject to a vesting repurchase right) works best when FMV is near par, so the purchase price is trivial. With a timely 83(b) election, there is no tax at vesting, all appreciation is capital gain, and the § 1202 (QSBS) holding period starts at issuance. Stock issued in exchange for services can be QSBS (§ 1202(c)(1)(B)(ii)). Under Pub. L. 119-21 (2025), QSBS acquired after July 4, 2025 qualifies for a 50% exclusion after three years, 75% after four, and 100% after five, subject to the per-issuer cap. The plan or a stand-alone restricted stock purchase agreement governs.
- *NSO* is better once FMV is material: the Advisor pays nothing until exercise. At exercise, the spread is ordinary income (reported on Form 1099-NEC for a non-employee), and the QSBS holding period starts only on exercise.
- Never grant "advisor shares" without Board approval and a written purchase or option agreement. Unapproved promises are a frequent diligence finding.

**Who owns advisor IP.** Advisors often hold day jobs (professors, executives) whose employers own their inventions. Section 2.5 and Section 9.1 require the Advisor to confirm that advising does not breach any employer or university policy, and to avoid using employer resources. University faculty in particular are typically bound by institutional patent policies; for a scientific advisor contributing to patentable work, obtain the institution's written acknowledgment or use a sponsored research or consulting arrangement approved by the institution.

**State-specific notes.**
- *California.* No employee non-solicit (Bus. & Prof. Code § 16600; AMN Healthcare, Inc. v. Aya Healthcare Services, Inc., 28 Cal. App. 5th 923 (2018)); the optional Section 11 is drafted as a narrow no-raid and should be deleted for a California Advisor. Advisors are rarely misclassification risks under the ABC test because they typically run their own established businesses or day jobs and give advice outside the usual course of the Company's business, but an "advisor" who works substantial hours on core product work for equity is likely an employee; use an offer letter instead.
- *Minnesota.* Minn. Stat. § 181.988 bans non-competes for independent contractors as well as employees. Do not add a non-compete.

**Common mistakes the form avoids.** Promising a fixed percentage instead of a share number (percentages change with every financing; Exhibit A states a share number); promising an exercise price; granting through an advisor's entity; omitting IP assignment and DTSA notice; leaving acceleration unaddressed; letting the agreement run indefinitely with no end date for vesting; and failing to document the Board grant.

**Deadlines triggered by signing.**
- Board approval of the grant (no statutory deadline, but grant promptly and obtain a current 409A valuation (Treas. Reg. § 1.409A-1(b)(5)(iv)(B)(2)(i) presumption: independent appraisal within 12 months, with no material intervening event)).
- § 83(b) election within 30 days after the restricted stock is issued (not after the Agreement is signed).
- California § 25102(o) notice (if applicable) within 30 days after the first issuance under the plan to a California resident.

# ADVISOR AGREEMENT

This Advisor Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of {{effective_date}} (the "**Effective Date**") by and between {{company_name}}, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and {{advisor_name}}, an individual (the "**Advisor**").

The Company wishes to obtain the Advisor's advice in the area of {{advisor_expertise_area}} and related matters, and the Advisor wishes to provide that advice, on the terms of this Agreement. In consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Company and the Advisor agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 "Board"** means the Board of Directors of the Company.

**1.2 "Change in Control"** has the meaning given to that term (or to "Corporate Transaction," "Sale Event," or the equivalent term) in the Plan.

**1.3 "Confidential Information"** has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

**1.4 "Equity Award"** means the award recommended under Section 3.1, if and as granted by the Board.

**1.5 "Intellectual Property Rights"** means all rights, anywhere in the world, in and to patents, patent applications, and statutory invention registrations (including continuations, continuations-in-part, divisionals, reissues, reexaminations, and extensions); copyrights and works of authorship (including software, documentation, and data compilations), whether or not registered; mask works; trade secrets and know-how; trademarks, service marks, and trade dress, together with the goodwill associated with them; moral rights; rights in designs; and all other intellectual or industrial property rights, together with all registrations, applications, renewals, and extensions of any of the foregoing and all rights to sue for past, present, and future infringement or misappropriation.

**1.6 "Plan"** means the Company's {{plan_name}}, as amended from time to time, or any successor equity incentive plan under which the Equity Award is granted.

**1.7 "Service"** means the Advisor's provision of services to the Company under this Agreement or in any other capacity (including as an employee, director, or consultant) without interruption. A change in the capacity in which the Advisor provides services does not end the Advisor's Service so long as the Advisor continues to provide services to the Company in some capacity.

**1.8 "Services"** has the meaning given in Section 2.1.

**1.9 "Work Product"** means all inventions (whether or not patentable), discoveries, improvements, ideas, designs, processes, formulas, data, software, works of authorship, and other materials, and all Intellectual Property Rights in them, that the Advisor, alone or jointly with others, conceives, reduces to practice, creates, or develops (a) in performing the Services or (b) through use of the Company's Confidential Information, in each case during the term of this Agreement.

## 2. Advisory Services

**2.1 Services.** During the term of this Agreement, the Advisor shall provide the advisory services described for the Advisor's engagement level ({{advisor_engagement_level}}) in Exhibit A (the "**Services**"), and other services the Company and the Advisor agree to in writing from time to time. The Company expects the Services to require approximately {{advisor_hours_per_month}} hours per month on average; the Advisor is not required to provide more time than that without the Advisor's agreement.

**2.2 Manner of Performance.** The Advisor shall perform the Services in a professional manner and in accordance with applicable law. The Advisor determines the method, details, and means of performing the Services, and the time and place of performance, subject to reasonable coordination with the Company's schedule.

**2.3 No Authority to Bind.** The Advisor has no authority to enter into any contract, incur any obligation, or make any representation on behalf of the Company, and shall not hold the Advisor out as having that authority. The Advisor's title, if any (such as "Advisor" or "Member, Scientific Advisory Board"), is honorary and does not make the Advisor an officer, director, or employee of the Company.

**2.4 Other Activities.** The Company acknowledges that the Advisor may be employed by, or provide services to, other persons during the term of this Agreement. The Advisor shall not, during the term, provide advisory or consulting services to any person that the Advisor knows is developing or marketing products or services that directly compete with the Company's products or services under active development, without first notifying the Company in writing so that the parties can discuss whether the engagement creates a conflict. In all cases, the Advisor shall not use or disclose Confidential Information in providing services to any other person.

> **Drafting note:** Section 2.4 is a notice-and-discuss conflict provision, not a non-compete. It is enforceable in most states because it operates only during the term and does not restrain the Advisor after the relationship ends. In California and Minnesota, keep it as a notice provision only; do not convert it into a prohibition.

**2.5 Third-Party Obligations.** The Advisor shall not, in performing the Services, (a) use any facilities, equipment, funds, personnel, or confidential information of any current or former employer, academic institution, or other person to which the Advisor owes obligations, or (b) incorporate into any Work Product any material, invention, or information that the Advisor does not have the right to assign or license to the Company. If the Advisor is employed by or appointed at a university, hospital, research institute, government agency, or other organization that has policies governing outside activities or intellectual property, the Advisor shall comply with those policies and shall notify the Company in writing before the Services could implicate them.

**2.6 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Advisor for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket travel and other expenses incurred in performing the Services, if approved in writing by the Company in advance, within thirty (30) days after the Company receives the Advisor's invoice and supporting documentation in accordance with the Company's expense policy.

## 3. Equity Award

**3.1 Recommendation of Grant.** Promptly after the Effective Date, the Company's management shall recommend that the Board grant the Advisor, under the Plan, the following Equity Award:

[[Alternative A: the right to purchase {{advisor_shares}} shares of the Company's Common Stock (the "**Restricted Shares**") at a purchase price per share equal to the fair market value of a share of Common Stock on the date of grant, as determined by the Board, under the Company's standard form of restricted stock purchase agreement under the Plan.]]

[[Alternative B: a non-qualified stock option to purchase {{advisor_shares}} shares of the Company's Common Stock, with an exercise price per share equal to the fair market value of a share of Common Stock on the date of grant, as determined by the Board, under the Company's standard form of stock option agreement under the Plan.]]

The Equity Award is subject to approval by the Board in its sole discretion, to the terms of the Plan, and to the Advisor's execution of the applicable award agreement. Nothing in this Agreement is a promise of any particular exercise or purchase price, and the Advisor acknowledges that the fair market value of the Common Stock may be higher at the date of grant than at the Effective Date. If the Board does not approve the grant within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, either party may terminate this Agreement under Section 4.2.

**3.2 Vesting.** The Equity Award shall vest as follows, subject to the Advisor's continued Service through each vesting date: [[Optional: no portion of the Equity Award vests before the date that is {{cliff_months}} months after {{vesting_commencement_date}} (the "**Vesting Commencement Date**"), on which date {{cliff_months}}/{{vesting_months}} of the shares subject to the Equity Award shall vest, and thereafter]] 1/{{vesting_months}} of the shares subject to the Equity Award shall vest on each monthly anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date [[Optional: following the first vesting date]], so that the Equity Award is fully vested on the date that is {{vesting_months}} months after the Vesting Commencement Date. If a monthly anniversary would fall on a day that does not exist in a month, it falls on the last day of that month.

> **Drafting note:** If the cliff is deleted, define the Vesting Commencement Date in the remaining sentence ("1/{{vesting_months}} of the shares … on each monthly anniversary of {{vesting_commencement_date}} (the "**Vesting Commencement Date**")"). Round fractional shares down monthly and true up at the final vesting date, consistent with the Plan's award agreement.

**3.3 Change in Control.**

[[Alternative A: Upon the consummation of a Change in Control, if the Advisor's Service has not ended before it, one hundred percent (100%) of the then-unvested shares subject to the Equity Award shall vest.]]

[[Alternative B: If, within the period beginning three (3) months before and ending twelve (12) months after the consummation of a Change in Control, the Company (or its successor) terminates this Agreement or the Advisor's Service other than for Cause, then, effective on the later of the termination date and the consummation of the Change in Control, one hundred percent (100%) of the then-unvested shares subject to the Equity Award shall vest. For this purpose, "**Cause**" means the Advisor's (i) material breach of Section 5, Section 7, or Section 9, (ii) commission of fraud, embezzlement, or a felony, or (iii) willful misconduct that materially harms the Company, in each case as determined by the Board in good faith. Any shares that remain unvested at the consummation of the Change in Control shall remain outstanding (or be assumed or substituted by the successor) until the end of the three-month period after termination referred to in this Section 3.3 so that this acceleration can take effect.]]

[[Alternative C: The Equity Award is not subject to accelerated vesting upon a Change in Control, and its treatment in a Change in Control is governed solely by the Plan.]]

> **Drafting note:** Alternative A (full single-trigger) is common for advisors because an acquirer rarely continues the advisory relationship, so double-trigger protection is illusory. Investors rarely object to single-trigger acceleration on small advisor grants. Alternative B (double-trigger) is used where the advisor relationship is expected to continue after an acquisition (e.g., a scientific advisor to a platform that the acquirer will keep). If Alternative B is chosen with options, confirm that the Plan does not terminate unassumed options at closing before the acceleration can operate; the last sentence addresses this for assumed awards only.

**3.4 Effect of End of Service.** Vesting ceases on the date the Advisor's Service ends for any reason.

[[Alternative A: The Company shall have the right to repurchase any Restricted Shares that are unvested on that date at the lower of the original purchase price and the then-current fair market value, within the period and on the terms set out in the restricted stock purchase agreement.]]

[[Alternative B: Any portion of the option that is unvested on that date shall terminate immediately, and the Advisor may exercise the vested portion until the earlier of (i) the date that is {{ptep_months}} months after the date the Advisor's Service ends and (ii) the option's expiration date, in each case as set out in the stock option agreement, except that the vested portion terminates immediately if this Agreement is terminated for breach of Section 5, Section 7, or Section 9.]]

**3.5 Securities Law Matters.** The Advisor represents that the Advisor is a natural person and that none of the Services will be provided in connection with the offer or sale of the Company's securities in a capital-raising transaction or will directly or indirectly promote or maintain a market for the Company's securities. The Advisor shall not, for compensation from the Company, introduce investors to the Company or participate in negotiating any investment in the Company. The Advisor acknowledges that the Equity Award and any shares issued under it have not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or any state securities law, are being issued in reliance on exemptions from registration, and are subject to restrictions on transfer under the Plan, the award agreement, the Company's bylaws, and applicable law.

**3.6 Taxes.** The Advisor is solely responsible for all taxes arising from the Equity Award and from any other payment under this Agreement. The Company has made no representation concerning the tax consequences of the Equity Award, and the Advisor has had the opportunity to consult the Advisor's own tax advisor. [[Optional: The Advisor acknowledges that if the Advisor wishes to make an election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, with respect to the Restricted Shares, the election must be filed with the Internal Revenue Service within thirty (30) days after the Restricted Shares are issued, that filing the election is solely the Advisor's responsibility even if the Advisor requests the Company's assistance, and that the Advisor shall deliver a copy of the filed election to the Company.]]

**3.7 No Other Compensation.** The Equity Award and the expense reimbursement described in Section 2.6 are the Advisor's sole compensation for the Services. The Advisor is not entitled to any salary, fee, bonus, commission, benefit, or other compensation for the Services.

## 4. Term and Termination

**4.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for {{term_months}} months, unless terminated earlier under Section 4.2. The parties may extend the term by written agreement (email sufficing).

**4.2 Termination.** Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, on ten (10) days' written notice to the other party. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party materially breaches it.

**4.3 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement, (a) the Advisor shall cease performing Services, and the Advisor's Service ends unless the Advisor continues to provide services to the Company in another capacity; (b) the Company shall reimburse expenses properly incurred before the termination date under Section 2.6; (c) the Equity Award is governed by Sections 3.3 and 3.4, the Plan, and the award agreement; and (d) the Advisor shall comply with Section 5.6.

**4.4 Survival.** Sections 3.3 through 3.6 and 4.3, this Section 4.4, and Articles 5 through 8, 10, and 12 [[Optional: and Article 11]] survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 5. Confidential Information

**5.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, that the Advisor obtains from or on behalf of the Company or its affiliates, or develops in performing the Services, including technical information (such as inventions, software, source code, algorithms, models, data, designs, research, and know-how), product plans and roadmaps, business plans and strategies, financial information and projections, capitalization and financing information, customer, supplier, and partner information, personnel information, and Work Product, and all non-public information of third parties that the Company is obligated to keep confidential.

**5.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Advisor can demonstrate by written evidence (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was known to the Advisor, without an obligation of confidentiality, before the Company disclosed it; or (c) is received by the Advisor on a non-confidential basis from a third party lawfully entitled to disclose it.

**5.3 Obligations.** The Advisor shall (a) use Confidential Information solely to perform the Services; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any person without the Company's prior written consent, except as permitted by Section 5.4 or Article 6; (c) protect Confidential Information with at least reasonable care; and (d) not input Confidential Information into any generative artificial-intelligence tool or other third-party service that may retain it, use it to train any model, or disclose it, unless the Company has approved the tool in writing.

**5.4 Compelled Disclosure.** If the Advisor is required by law to disclose Confidential Information, the Advisor shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Company prompt written notice and reasonable cooperation so that the Company may seek a protective order, and shall disclose only what the Advisor's counsel advises is legally required.

**5.5 Duration.**

[[Alternative A: The Advisor's obligations under this Article 5 continue during the term of this Agreement and at all times thereafter.]]

[[Alternative B: The Advisor's obligations under this Article 5 continue during the term of this Agreement and for {{confidentiality_years}} years thereafter, except that they continue with respect to any trade secret of the Company for as long as it remains a trade secret under applicable law.]]

**5.6 Return of Materials.** On the Company's request, and in any event on expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Advisor shall promptly return to the Company or destroy (and permanently delete from the Advisor's personal devices and accounts) all Confidential Information and Work Product in the Advisor's possession or control, and on request shall certify in writing that the Advisor has done so.

## 6. Protected Disclosures; Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice

**6.1 Protected Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits or restricts the Advisor from (a) reporting a possible violation of law to, filing a charge or complaint with, communicating with, or participating in an investigation or proceeding conducted by any federal, state, or local governmental agency or commission, including the Securities and Exchange Commission, without notice to or consent from the Company; (b) receiving an award for information provided to any governmental agency; (c) testifying truthfully in any legal proceeding; or (d) disclosing information about sexual assault, sexual harassment, discrimination, or other conduct the Advisor reasonably believes is unlawful, to the extent such a disclosure is protected by law.

**6.2 Defend Trade Secrets Act Notice.** Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that (a) is made (i) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, and (ii) solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or (b) is made in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if such filing is made under seal. An individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation by an employer for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the individual's attorney and use the trade secret information in the court proceeding, if the individual files any document containing the trade secret under seal and does not disclose the trade secret, except pursuant to court order.

## 7. Intellectual Property

**7.1 Disclosure.** The Advisor shall promptly disclose to the Company in writing all Work Product.
