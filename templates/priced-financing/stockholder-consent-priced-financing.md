## About this template

**What it does.** Delaware lets stockholders act without a meeting by signed consents from holders of at least the votes that would be needed at a meeting where all shares were present (§ 228(a)), unless the charter forbids it. This form collects every stockholder approval a priced round needs in one instrument with separate signature blocks for each voting group, so that the Company can prove each required vote: (1) adoption of the Restated Certificate under §§ 242 and 245; (2) approval of the option pool increase (for ISO status); (3) waivers under existing investor agreements (pro rata rights, notice periods, any right of first refusal); and, if useful, (4) disinterested-stockholder approval of an insider-led round under § 144(a)(2). Exhibit A is the § 228(e) notice; Exhibit B is the post-closing consent electing the Series Directors.

**Which votes are required.** Build a vote table for every deal from the current charter and agreements:

| Approval | Who votes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Restated Certificate | Majority of the outstanding stock entitled to vote, voting together | § 242(b)(1) |
| Increase in authorized Common Stock | Majority of the outstanding Common Stock as a separate class, **unless** the current charter already contains a § 242(b)(2) opt-out for the Common | § 242(b)(2) |
| Adopting a § 242(b)(2) opt-out in the Restated Certificate | Majority of the outstanding Common Stock (so the opt-out is valid for future increases) | § 242(b)(2), last sentence |
| Changes that adversely affect an existing class or series' powers, preferences or special rights | Majority of that class (or series, if only that series is adversely affected) | § 242(b)(2) |
| Existing preferred protective provisions (creating a senior or pari passu series, amending the charter, increasing authorized shares) | The threshold in the current charter (e.g., "Requisite Preferred Holders" or a series vote) | Current charter |
| Waiver of pro rata rights and notices under a prior IRA or side letter | The waiver threshold in that agreement | Prior agreements |
| Plan share increase | Approval of stockholders within 12 months; the plan may specify the vote (commonly a majority of votes cast or outstanding) | § 422(b)(1); Treas. Reg. § 1.422-2(b) |

Delaware cases generally hold that creating a new senior series does not by itself "alter or change the powers, preferences, or special rights" of an existing class for § 242(b)(2) purposes, because the existing class's own rights are unchanged; existing preferred investors protect themselves through charter protective provisions, which is why the protective-provision vote row matters. If the current charter is unusual, analyze it rather than relying on the table.

**§ 242(d) and the 2026 amendments.** § 242(d) (2023) lets a company with a class listed on a national securities exchange change authorized shares of that class with a "votes cast" standard; it does not apply to a private company's financing. H.B. 353 (85 Del. Laws c. 279, effective August 1, 2026) clarified that a charter provision opting out of the § 242(b)(2) class vote is not, by itself, an express requirement that displaces § 242(d). Relevant to the charter drafter at the IPO, not to this consent.

**Record date and timing.** If the board did not fix a record date and prior board action is required (as it is for a charter amendment), the record date for consents is the close of business on the day the board adopted its resolutions (§ 213(b)). Only holders of record on that date may consent. The required number of signed consents must be delivered within 60 days after the first consent is delivered (§ 228(c)). A consent is revocable until it becomes effective. A signer may make a consent effective at a later time, not more than 60 days after the instruction, if the Company receives evidence of that instruction (§ 228(c)); this form uses that mechanism to fix a definite Effective Time. Consents may be delivered electronically to an information processing system the Company designates, if the consent shows the date of delivery and the signer's identity (§ 228(d)(1)(iv)).

**Notice to non-consenting holders.** If the action is taken by less than unanimous consent, the Company must give "prompt notice" to the stockholders who did not consent and who would have been entitled to notice of a meeting (§ 228(e)). Send it with or immediately after the closing; Exhibit A is a form. The certificate filed with the Secretary of State must state that consent was given under § 228 in place of any statement about a meeting vote (§ 228(e)).

**Disclosure to stockholders.** When a board seeks stockholder action, directors must disclose fully and fairly all material information within the board's control. Attach the Restated Certificate, summarize the financing terms, the dilution, and any director's or insider's interest, and send the package to every stockholder whose consent is sought. A consent obtained with a materially incomplete package can be challenged, and the § 144(a)(2) safe harbor requires an *informed* vote.

**Common mistakes.** Missing the Common class vote when the existing charter has no § 242(b)(2) opt-out; forgetting the existing series' protective-provision vote; obtaining consents from holders who were not holders on the record date; letting the 60-day window lapse; filing the Restated Certificate before the required consents are delivered; and forgetting the § 228(e) notice.

**Effective Time: 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time on {{stockholder_consent_effective_date}}**

The undersigned holders of the outstanding capital stock of {{company_name}}, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), acting without a meeting under Section 228 of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**"), consent to the adoption of the resolutions below. The undersigned include, in each case as of the close of business on {{board_consent_date}} (the record date under Section 213(b) of the DGCL):

(a) holders of a majority of the voting power of all outstanding shares of the Company's capital stock entitled to vote, voting together as a single class [[Optional: on an as-converted basis]];

(b) holders of a majority of the outstanding shares of Common Stock, voting as a separate class; [[Optional: and

(c) holders of the shares of {{prior_series_name}} Preferred Stock required by the Company's current Certificate of Incorporation and by Section 242(b)(2) of the DGCL to approve the matters below, voting as a separate [[Alternative A: class]] [[Alternative B: series]]]].

Each signatory (i) directs that this consent become effective at the Effective Time stated above, which is not more than sixty (60) days after the date of that signatory's instruction, (ii) agrees that it may revoke this consent only by written notice received by the Company before the Effective Time, and (iii) agrees that the Company may treat a signature delivered by email to {{company_notice_email}} or through the Company's electronic signature platform as delivery under Section 228(d) of the DGCL. ## 1. AMENDED AND RESTATED CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION

WHEREAS, on {{board_consent_date}} the Board of Directors of the Company (the "**Board**") adopted resolutions setting forth, declaring advisable and recommending to the stockholders the Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation attached as Exhibit C (the "**Restated Certificate**"), which, among other things, creates the {{series_name}} Preferred Stock [[Optional: and a shadow series of Preferred Stock issuable on conversion of the Company's SAFEs and convertible notes]], increases the Company's authorized capital stock to {{authorized_common_shares}} shares of Common Stock and {{authorized_preferred_shares}} shares of Preferred Stock, and sets out the rights, preferences, privileges and restrictions of each series of Preferred Stock; and

## 2. INCREASE IN THE EQUITY INCENTIVE PLAN RESERVE

RESOLVED, that the amendment to the {{plan_name}} adopted by the Board on {{board_consent_date}}, increasing the number of shares of Common Stock reserved for issuance under it by {{plan_pool_increase_shares}} shares to a total of {{plan_reserve_shares}} shares, and increasing the maximum number of shares issuable as incentive stock options to the same total, is approved for all purposes, including Section 422 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and the related Treasury Regulations.

## 3. WAIVER OF NOTICE

RESOLVED, that each undersigned stockholder waives, under Section 229 of the DGCL and any applicable provision of the Company's Bylaws, any notice of the matters approved by this consent to which that stockholder would otherwise be entitled, including any notice of a meeting of stockholders, and consents to receive any notice under the DGCL by electronic transmission at the email address the stockholder has on file with the Company, as permitted by Section 232 of the DGCL.

## 4. WAIVERS UNDER EXISTING INVESTOR AGREEMENTS

[[Optional: RESOLVED, that the undersigned holders party to the {{prior_agreement_title}} dated {{prior_agreement_date}} (the "**Prior Agreement**"), constituting at least the {{prior_waiver_threshold}} required under the Prior Agreement to waive its provisions, waive, for themselves and for every other party entitled to those rights, (a) any right of first offer, preemptive right, participation right or notice right under the Prior Agreement or any side letter with respect to the issuance of {{series_name}} Preferred Stock at the Initial Closing and any Additional Closing under the {{series_name}} Preferred Stock Purchase Agreement, the issuance of shares on conversion of the Company's SAFEs and convertible notes, and the issuance of Common Stock on conversion of any of those shares; and (b) any notice period or other procedural requirement under the Prior Agreement relating to the Financing (as defined in the Board's resolutions); and consent to the amendment and restatement of the Prior Agreement in the form delivered at the Initial Closing.]]

> **Drafting note:** Use the waiver threshold and mechanics in the actual prior agreement. Many seed-stage agreements let a majority waive pro rata rights for everyone, but some require each holder's consent or prohibit waivers that favor participating insiders. Investors that actually participate in the round often still want the waiver so that their participation does not have to follow the prior agreement's offer procedure.

## 6. DISINTERESTED STOCKHOLDER APPROVAL

[[Optional: WHEREAS, the material facts relating to the interests of certain directors and stockholders in the Financing, including the interests described in the information statement delivered with this consent, have been disclosed to the undersigned stockholders;

RESOLVED, that the undersigned stockholders who have no material interest in the Financing and no material relationship with any Person that has such an interest (as those terms are defined in Section 144(e) of the DGCL) approve and ratify the Financing and the Transaction Agreements for purposes of Section 144(a)(2) [[Optional: and Section 144(b)(2)]] of the DGCL.]]

> **Verify:** § 144(a)(2) refers to "an informed, uncoerced, affirmative vote of a majority of the votes cast by the disinterested stockholders." Confirm with Delaware counsel how to measure "votes cast" when approval is by written consent (practice is to require consents from a majority of the outstanding shares held by disinterested stockholders), and, for a controlling-stockholder transaction under § 144(b)(2), that the Financing was conditioned on that approval when first submitted to stockholders.

## 7. GENERAL

RESOLVED, that the officers of the Company are authorized to take any action and sign any document they consider necessary or appropriate to carry out these resolutions, and any action already taken consistent with them is ratified.
