## About this template

**What it does.** This package contains everything an optionholder signs or receives: (1) the **Notice of Stock Option Grant** (the "Grant Notice," the one-page summary of economic terms); (2) the **Stock Option Agreement** (Exhibit A, the standard terms, which are the same for every holder); (3) the **Exercise Notice and Stock Purchase Agreement** (Exhibit B, signed at exercise), including an early-exercise alternative with a repurchase option on Unvested Shares; and supporting exhibits: **Joint Escrow Instructions** (Exhibit C), **Assignment Separate from Certificate** (Exhibit D), **Spousal Consent** (Exhibit E), and a pointer to the **Section 83(b) election** (Exhibit F). The plan controls over the package (Plan § 16.16).

**Sequence.** The Board (or a delegate under Plan § 3.4) approves the grant in a consent that fixes the number of shares, the exercise price (not less than FMV on the **actual** Grant Date), ISO/NSO status, and vesting ([board-consent-equity-grants](board-consent-equity-grants.md)). The Company then delivers the Grant Notice, the Option Agreement, the Plan, and (if required) Rule 701(e) information. The holder accepts the grant by signing (electronically is fine). At exercise, the holder delivers Exhibit B with payment; on early exercise, also Exhibits C, D, and E and a copy of the 83(b) election within 30 days.

### Requirements this package satisfies

- **ISO terms** (I.R.C. § 422(b)): granted under a stockholder-approved plan; exercise price ≥ FMV (110% for a Ten Percent Stockholder, with a 5-year term under § 422(c)(5)); term ≤ 10 years; nontransferable except by will or descent; ISO status lost for exercises more than 3 months after employment ends (1 year for § 22(e)(3) disability), § 422(a)(2), (c)(6); $100,000 first-exercisable limit applied in grant order, § 422(d) and Treas. Reg. § 1.422-4.
- **Disqualifying disposition notice.** The Company needs to know about sales within 2 years of grant or 1 year of exercise (§ 422(a)(1)) to report compensation income on Form W-2 and claim its deduction. The holder covenants to notify the Company (Option Agreement § 5.3; Exercise Agreement § 9).
- **§ 409A.** The option is exempt only if the price is at least FMV on the grant date and the option has no deferral feature (Treas. Reg. § 1.409A-1(b)(5)(i)(A)). The Grant Notice records the 409A valuation relied on so the file shows the price basis.
- **Rule 701 and California.** The Company delivers the Plan with the grant (Rule 701(e)); if 12-month Rule 701 sales exceed $10 million, it also delivers financial statements no older than 180 days and risk factors a reasonable time before **exercise** (Rule 701(e)(6)). California holders get at least 30 days to exercise after termination (6 months after death or disability), and the option term cannot exceed 120 months (10 CCR § 260.140.41(b), (e)).
- **Early exercise.** Shares bought before vesting are "Unvested Shares" subject to a Company repurchase option at the lower of cost or FMV; the holder must decide within 30 days whether to file an 83(b) election (Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2(b)). The 83(b) election may be filed on IRS Form 15620 by mail or, since July 2025, online through an IRS account (secondary sources; confirm current procedure). The holder must give the Company a copy (Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2(d)).
- **Form 3921.** For each ISO exercise, the Company must furnish Form 3921 to the holder by January 31 of the following year and file it with the IRS by the end of February (paper) or March 31 (electronic); e-filing is mandatory when the Company files 10 or more information returns of all types in the aggregate (IRS Instructions for Forms 3921 and 3922, Rev. 04/2025).

### State-specific notes

- **Community property states** (Arizona, California, Idaho, Louisiana, Nevada, New Mexico, Texas, Washington, Wisconsin; and elective regimes in a few others): obtain a spousal consent at exercise (and at grant for executives) so the spouse is bound by the repurchase option, ROFR, and market stand-off. A consent is not required for validity of the option but avoids disputes in divorce or death.
- **California**: add the California notice in the Grant Notice; keep the PTEP floors; consider Cal. Lab. Code § 925 for California employees (see Option Agreement § 14.2 drafting note).
- **Non-U.S. holders**: attach a country addendum (Plan § 5.6).

### Common mistakes

1. Using a stale or superseded 409A value, or pricing at the value on the offer-letter date rather than the actual Grant Date.
2. Calling the grant an ISO for a consultant, non-employee director, or employee of a non-corporate affiliate. Those must be NSOs.
3. Leaving the $100,000 limit unchecked on large or early-exercisable grants; the excess is automatically an NSO, but the Grant Notice should say so to avoid surprise.
4. Allowing early exercise without the escrow and repurchase mechanics, or without telling the holder that the 83(b) clock runs from the exercise date.
5. Mismatched vesting commencement date and Grant Date. The vesting commencement date may precede the Grant Date (for example, the start date), but the exercise price is always fixed on the Grant Date.

## EXHIBIT A — STOCK OPTION AGREEMENT

# {{company_name}}
# {{plan_name}}
# STOCK OPTION AGREEMENT

As reflected in your Notice of Stock Option Grant (the "**Grant Notice**"), {{company_name}} (the "**Company**") has granted you an option under its {{plan_name}} (the "**Plan**") to purchase the number of shares of Common Stock at the exercise price stated in the Grant Notice (the "**Option**"). Capitalized terms not defined in this Stock Option Agreement (this "**Agreement**") or the Grant Notice have the meanings given in the Plan. The terms of the Option, in addition to those in the Grant Notice and the Plan, are as follows.

## 1. GRANT OF THE OPTION

**1.1 Grant.** The Company grants you the Option on the Grant Date for the number of shares, at the Exercise Price, and of the type stated in the Grant Notice. The Option is granted under the Plan and is subject to all of its terms, which are incorporated in this Agreement by reference.

**1.2 Option Type.** If the Grant Notice designates the Option as an Incentive Stock Option, the Option is intended to qualify as an Incentive Stock Option under Section 422 of the Code to the maximum extent permitted, but the Company does not represent or guarantee that it will. Any portion of the Option that does not qualify (including because of the $100,000 limit in Section 422(d) of the Code, the timing of your exercise after employment ends, or a later modification) will be a Nonstatutory Stock Option. If the Grant Notice designates the Option as a Nonstatutory Stock Option, it is not intended to qualify as an Incentive Stock Option.

**1.3 Ten Percent Stockholder.** If you are a Ten Percent Stockholder on the Grant Date and the Option is designated an Incentive Stock Option, the Exercise Price is at least one hundred ten percent (110%) of the Fair Market Value on the Grant Date and the Option expires no later than five (5) years after the Grant Date. If the Grant Notice does not satisfy these conditions, the Option is a Nonstatutory Stock Option.

## 2. VESTING

**2.1 Vesting Schedule.** The Option vests in accordance with the vesting schedule in the Grant Notice, subject to your Continuous Service through each vesting date. Vesting ceases on the date your Continuous Service ends, and the unvested portion of the Option is forfeited on that date, unless the Plan, the Grant Notice, or another written agreement between you and the Company expressly provides otherwise.

**2.2 Corporate Transactions and Changes in Capitalization.** The number of shares subject to the Option and the Exercise Price will be adjusted for Capitalization Adjustments as provided in Section 11.1 of the Plan. In a Corporate Transaction, the Option will be treated as provided in Section 11.3 of the Plan (and Section 11.4 of the Plan, if included), which permits, without your consent, assumption, continuation, or substitution of the Option, cash-out (including cancellation of the Option for no payment if the per-share consideration does not exceed the Exercise Price), or termination of the Option after notice and an opportunity to exercise. Any acceleration of vesting is governed by the Plan and by any written agreement with you that expressly provides for it.

**2.3 Leaves and Changes in Time Commitment.** Vesting during leaves of absence and following a reduction in your time commitment is governed by Sections 16.4 and 16.5 of the Plan.

## 3. EXERCISE

**3.1 Exercise of Vested Option.** You may exercise the vested portion of the Option at any time before it terminates by delivering to the Company (or its designated equity administrator) a completed and signed Exercise Notice and Stock Purchase Agreement in the form of Exhibit B or another form the Company approves (including an electronic exercise procedure), payment of the aggregate Exercise Price for the shares being purchased, satisfaction of any Withholding Obligation, and any other documents the Company reasonably requires (including, if you are married or in a registered domestic partnership and reside in a community property jurisdiction, a spousal consent). You may not exercise the Option for a fraction of a share or for fewer than one hundred (100) shares (or the remaining vested shares, if fewer).

**3.2 Early Exercise.** [[Alternative A: Early exercise is not permitted. You may exercise the Option only for vested shares.]] [[Alternative B: If the Grant Notice permits early exercise, you may exercise all or any part of the Option, including the unvested portion, at any time before it terminates. The Company may satisfy any Withholding Obligation by any method permitted by Section 13.1 of the Plan. The Company may refuse to honor an exercise and to deliver shares if the Withholding Obligation is not satisfied.

**3.5 Compliance with Law.** You may not exercise the Option unless the shares issuable on exercise are then registered under the Securities Act or, if not registered, the Company has determined that the exercise and issuance would be exempt from registration. The exercise must also comply with all other Applicable Law, including, if applicable, the Company's receipt of the information described in Rule 701(e) a reasonable period of time before exercise and your agreement to keep that information confidential (Section 10.3 of the Plan).

**3.6 Investment Intent and Transfer Restrictions.** On exercise, you must sign the Exercise Notice and Stock Purchase Agreement, which includes investment representations and your agreement to the right of first refusal, market stand-off, and other restrictions in Section 12 of the Plan and in any stockholder agreement described in Section 10.7 of the Plan.

**3.7 Blackout Periods.** The Administrator may suspend your right to exercise the Option for up to thirty (30) days before a Corporate Transaction or other event described in Section 3.2(e) of the Plan, and, after an IPO, during any blackout period under the Company's insider trading policy.

**4.2 Unvested Portion.** The unvested portion of the Option terminates on the date your Continuous Service ends, unless the Plan or a written agreement with you provides otherwise.

**4.4 California Minimum Periods.** If you were a resident of California on the Grant Date, the periods in Section 4.1(b), (c), and (d) will never be shorter than thirty (30) days (for clause (b)) or six (6) months (for clauses (c) and (d)), but the Option will never be exercisable after the Expiration Date.

**4.5 No Notice of Expiration.** The Company has no obligation to notify you of the termination or expiration of the Option. You are responsible for tracking the dates in this Section 4.

## 5. INCENTIVE STOCK OPTION PROVISIONS

**5.1 $100,000 Limit.** To the extent that the aggregate Fair Market Value (measured on the Grant Date) of the shares for which the Option and all other incentive stock options granted to you by the Company or any Affiliate first become exercisable during any calendar year exceeds one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000), the excess will be treated as a Nonstatutory Stock Option, applying options in the order they were granted under Treasury Regulations Section 1.422-4. If the Option permits early exercise, all shares that may be purchased on early exercise are treated as first exercisable in the calendar year of the Grant Date. If vesting is accelerated, the accelerated shares are treated as first exercisable in the year of acceleration. The Company will designate which shares are acquired under the Incentive Stock Option portion and which under the Nonstatutory Stock Option portion, and will record that designation in its stock records.

**5.2 Holding Periods.** To receive Incentive Stock Option tax treatment on a sale of the shares, you must not dispose of the shares within two (2) years after the Grant Date or within one (1) year after the date the shares are transferred to you on exercise.

**5.3 Notice of Disqualifying Disposition.** If you sell or otherwise dispose of any shares acquired under an Incentive Stock Option on or before the later of (a) the second (2nd) anniversary of the Grant Date and (b) the first (1st) anniversary of the date the shares were transferred to you (a "**Disqualifying Disposition**"), you shall notify the Company in writing within fifteen (15) days after the date of the disposition, stating the date of disposition, the number of shares disposed of, the amount realized, and the manner of disposition. You agree that the Company may require shares acquired under an Incentive Stock Option to be held in a book-entry account or certificated with a legend identifying them as Incentive Stock Option shares until the end of those periods, and that you may be subject to income tax withholding by the Company on the compensation income you recognize.

**5.4 Alternative Minimum Tax.** You acknowledge that the excess of the Fair Market Value of the shares on the exercise date over the Exercise Price of an Incentive Stock Option is an item of adjustment for alternative minimum tax purposes, and that the exercise may cause you to owe alternative minimum tax even if you do not sell the shares.

**5.5 Information Return.** The Company will furnish you a Form 3921 (or successor) for each calendar year in which you exercise any portion of the Option that is an Incentive Stock Option.

## 6. TRANSFERABILITY

## EXHIBIT F — SECTION 83(b) ELECTION

Use the Section 83(b) election form in [83b-election-package](../founders/83b-election-package.md) (or IRS Form 15620), completed for the Unvested Shares purchased on early exercise. The election must state the information required by Treasury Regulations Section 1.83-2(e): the taxpayer's name, address, and taxpayer identification number; a description of the property; the date of transfer and the taxable year; the nature of the restrictions (here, the Repurchase Option); the fair market value at transfer, determined without regard to lapse restrictions (normally equal to the Exercise Price for an option priced at FMV); the amount paid; and a statement that copies have been furnished as required. It must be filed with the IRS within thirty (30) days after the Exercise Date (by mail, preferably certified with return receipt, or online if available), and a copy must be delivered to the Company.

> **Drafting note:** The Company should calendar the 30-day deadline for each early exercise and remind the holder in writing, while making clear the filing is the holder's responsibility. For ISO early exercises, the election matters only for AMT purposes; for NSO early exercises at FMV, it fixes ordinary income at zero. If the other drafter's 83(b) package lives at a different path, update the link.

> **Verify:** Confirm the IRS's current online filing procedure for Form 15620 (reported by multiple law firms as available since July 2025) before advising holders on electronic filing.
